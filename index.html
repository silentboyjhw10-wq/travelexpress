Before you fly

Your pre-trip checklist

Most delays at the airport come from missing basics. Check these a few weeks before you travel, and ask us if anything is unclear.

  • Passport validityMany countries want at least six months of validity left, and blank pages for stamps.
  • Visa or entry permissionCheck the rules for your destination and your transit country.
  • Names match your passportTickets and hotel bookings must carry the name exactly as printed in the passport.
  • Insurance and health papersSome countries require travel insurance or vaccination records.
  • Copies of everythingKeep printed and phone copies of tickets, hotel bookings and your passport page.

Book with care

How to book travel safely

Travel fraud is common. These habits protect your money, whichever agency you use.

Get everything in writing

Ask for a written quote and a receipt for every payment, with the agency’s name and contact details on it.

Check your ticket yourself

Match your name and flight details with the airline’s own website or app using your booking reference.

Verify the agency

Ask to see the registration certificate, and confirm it with the authority before paying a large amount.

Beware of “too good to be true”

Very low prices, pressure to pay today and requests to pay into a personal account are warning signs.

Never use a tourist visa to work

Be careful of anyone offering a job abroad on a tourist visa. Overseas work needs proper approval for the destination and from the Bangladeshi authorities.

Protect your documents

Share passport copies only with people you trust, and never share bank codes or OTPs with anyone.

Can you guarantee my visa?

No. Visa decisions are made by embassies and immigration authorities, so no agency can promise approval. We help you prepare a complete and correct application.

Which documents do I need?

It depends on the country and visa type. Commonly a valid passport, photographs and proof of your travel plans are needed. Tell us your trip and we will send you the exact checklist.

How and when do I pay?

The payment method and schedule are confirmed with you in writing before booking, and you should receive a receipt for every payment.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

It depends on the airline or hotel rules for your fare. We explain the change and refund conditions before you pay, so there are no surprises.
